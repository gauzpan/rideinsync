Why RideInSync

Ride together, stay together

See every rider's live position and status on one map, so you adjust your pace without panicking or calling anyone.

One tap for help

SOS alerts your group, your leader, and your emergency contact at once, with your location and medical info.

Eyes on the road

Voice-first signals for stops, hazards, and route changes. Minimal touch, minimal distraction while you ride.

No one left behind

Last-known location and a sweep view catch anyone who falls behind or splits off at a junction.
